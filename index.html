This is Razieh (Razi /ˈrɑːzi/ for short). I'm a PhD student researcher supervised by Prof. Hilde Kuehne at the University of Tuebingen, Germany. My research mainly involves reasoning and long-video context in multimodal LLMs.

I had the honor of collaborating with postdocs at Prof. Philip Torr's Vision Group at the University of Oxford, and also working at Prof. Zeynep Akata's EML lab at the Technical University of Munich as a visiting AI researcher. If you'd like to discuss ideas, feel free to reach out on LinkedIn or via email: razieh92rezaei@gmail.com.
